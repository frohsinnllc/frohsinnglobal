# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

International prospects evaluating Frohsinn LLC: small-business operators and teams considering the 48h Automation Quick Fix (599 EUR sprint), developers evaluating the Claude Code Operator Kit, and Chrome users discovering SlashPrompt / Chatport. They arrive from ads, the Chrome Web Store, or guides, usually on mobile, and decide within seconds whether this company is credible enough to pay.

## Product Purpose

frohsinnglobal.com is the corporate face of Frohsinn LLC (US-registered, international operations). It must (1) make the company look credible and real, (2) present the product portfolio in one ordered place, and (3) route buyers to the right checkout or store page with minimal friction. Success = visitors trust the entity and click through to a product or write to mail@frohsinnglobal.com.

## Brand Personality

Nüchtern, kompetent, verlässlich — sober, competent, reliable. The voice of a precise engineering partner: plain statements, concrete prices, no hype. Confidence through clarity, not through decoration.

## Anti-references

- Generic Tailwind-template SaaS landing pages (blue-600 buttons, stock "team at whiteboard" photos, icon-card grids).
- AI-slop tells: gradient text, glassmorphism, hero-metric blocks, tracked uppercase eyebrows above every section.
- Editorial-magazine affectation (display serifs, italic headlines) — wrong register for an engineering-services firm.
- Crypto/startup maximalism; anything loud, animated, or salesy.

## Design Principles

1. **Say the price.** Concrete numbers and deliverables up front; specificity is the trust signal.
2. **One idea per fold.** Sober pacing, generous whitespace, no competing CTAs.
3. **Navy is the brand.** Commit to the logo's deep navy as a carried surface color, not as a decoration on white.
4. **Real over stocky.** No generic stock photography; typography, structure and the products themselves carry the visual weight.
5. **Works on a phone first.** Buyers arrive from ads and the Chrome Web Store on mobile; navigation and CTAs must work at 375px.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG 2.1 AA: text contrast ≥ 4.5:1 (large text ≥ 3:1), full keyboard operability with visible focus, semantic landmarks and heading order, alt text on informative images, `prefers-reduced-motion` respected, touch targets ≥ 44px.
